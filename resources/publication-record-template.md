## Formatting Checks
- [ ] Author order matches the official record.
- [ ] Title and venue are exact.
- [ ] Year, volume, issue, and pages are verified.
- [ ] DOI resolves to the intended work.
- [ ] Publication status is current.
- [ ] Preprints and under-review work are not presented as published.
- [ ] One citation style is used consistently within the category.
