# Academic CV Final Review Checklist

## Accuracy
- [ ] Name, contact details, affiliations, and dates are correct.
- [ ] Degrees, institutions, and thesis details are accurate.
- [ ] Academic appointments and responsibilities are verifiable.
- [ ] Publication author order and status are correct.
- [ ] Grants identify the applicant's actual role and amount.
- [ ] Teaching, supervision, and service claims are accurate.
- [ ] Awards and memberships are verified.
- [ ] No placeholder details or invented achievements remain.

## Structure
- [ ] Section order reflects the target position.
- [ ] Most relevant evidence is easy to find.
- [ ] Publications are grouped consistently.
- [ ] Teaching and research contributions are distinct.
- [ ] Optional sections are included only when useful or requested.

## Formatting
- [ ] Dates use a consistent format.
- [ ] Headings, bullets, spacing, and typography are consistent.
- [ ] Citation style is consistent within each category.
- [ ] Long entries wrap cleanly.
- [ ] Links and DOIs work.
- [ ] PDF export preserves text and links.

## Application Fit
- [ ] The CV follows the institution's instructions.
- [ ] Required sections and declarations are present.
- [ ] Country- and discipline-specific conventions are respected.
- [ ] Length is appropriate for career stage and application.
- [ ] Sensitive personal details are limited to what is required.

## Final Proof
- [ ] Spellcheck completed.
- [ ] Reverse chronological order applied where appropriate.
- [ ] Gaps, overlaps, and date inconsistencies reviewed.
- [ ] Final PDF visually inspected.
